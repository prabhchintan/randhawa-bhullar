Randhawa for iOS

Randhawa shows the year as a grid of dots, one for every day, on your Home and Lock Screen. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Frequently asked

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, tap the + in the corner, search for Randhawa, choose a size, and tap Add. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a widget under the clock.

When does it update?

The widget advances on its own at midnight, so it always shows the correct day. Opening the app also refreshes it immediately.

Can it change my wallpaper automatically?

No app can. iOS does not allow apps to change your wallpaper. The widget is the built-in way to keep the year in view on your Home and Lock Screen.

Does it work with leap years?

Yes. Randhawa shows 366 dots automatically in a leap year and 365 otherwise.

Do you collect any data?

No. Randhawa collects nothing and makes no network connections. Read the privacy policy.
