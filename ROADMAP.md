# Roadmap

The apps move slowly on purpose. This is the honest list of what is in
flight, what comes next, what is being considered, and what will never
happen. Nothing here is a promise except the last section.

## In review now

- Randhawa 3.0: memories (a thought or photo pinned to a place), optional
  iCloud sync, the Map widget as the one widget
- Bhullar 2.0: memories in the grid, gold days, "on this day"

## Next

- Say "open source" inside the apps and on the App Store pages, and link
  back to this repository
- Gold memory dots in Bhullar's widgets
- Tap a memory dot on Randhawa's map to open that memory directly
- "On this day" surfaced in Randhawa's map view, not only in the list

## Considering

- Camera capture in the composer (today it is the system photo picker)
- iPad layouts that actually use the width
- Localization, starting with Punjabi
- A shared timeline view: every memory, place and time together

## Will not happen

- Accounts, passwords, or any sign-in of our own; the Apple Account is the
  account
- Analytics, tracking, or ads
- Servers of ours; sync lives in the user's private iCloud, which we cannot
  read
- Background location; location is read once per open, while the app is
  open, and never otherwise
