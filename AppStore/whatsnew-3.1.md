The trail.

Randhawa has always waited for you to open it. Now, if you want it to, it can keep drawing while you get on with your day.

Turn the trail on from the menu and pick a pace: whenever you move, at most every fifteen minutes, hourly, every four hours, or only when you arrive somewhere and stay a while. Your map fills in the hours you were too busy to reach for your phone.

It is off until you turn it on, and off again the moment you change your mind. A pace is a ceiling, not a schedule: iOS wakes the app when you move, never on a timer, so standing still adds nothing and your battery barely notices. And "Forget the trail" deletes every dot the trail placed while keeping every dot you placed yourself.

Where your places live has not changed at all. The same file on your phone, the same optional private iCloud that we cannot read, the same absence of servers of ours.

If you also use Bhullar, this is the half that makes its new feature work. Tap any dot in its grid and you will see where you were.
