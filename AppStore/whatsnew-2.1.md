# Randhawa 2.1: What's New (for App Store Connect)

New in 2.1: the Map widget.

Your moments, as orange dots on a real map, right on your Home Screen, denser where you return, your latest moment ringed in white. The original Constellation widget is still here; now you can have both.

As always: your location is read only while the app is open, stays on this device, and is never sent anywhere. The widget only draws moments you've already made. It never reads your location.
