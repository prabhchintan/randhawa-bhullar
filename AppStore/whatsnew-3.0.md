# Randhawa 3.0: What's New (for App Store Connect)

Randhawa 3.0 is about keeping what you make.

• Memories: tap the plus button to pin a thought, or a photo, to where you are. Memories live on your map in gold. If you also use Bhullar, our time app, memories flow between the two: made in either, shown in both. Randhawa gives them a place; Bhullar gives them a time.

• iCloud sync, off until you turn it on: your moments and memories are kept in your private iCloud, which we cannot read. Get a new phone, sign in, and your map comes back. No account to create; your Apple Account is the account.

• The Map widget now shows memories too.

• The Constellation widget has been retired. The constellation itself lives on inside the app, one tap from the map.

As always: location is read only while the app is open, and nothing about you ever reaches us.
