Privacy Policy · Randhawa & Bhullar · Last updated July 2026

Our apps — Randhawa and Bhullar — do not collect any data.

What we collect

Nothing. Neither app has user accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs. We operate no servers, and nothing about you is ever sent to us.

Location (Randhawa only)

Randhawa draws a personal map: each time you open the app, it marks a dot where you are. To do that, it reads your location once per open, only while the app is on screen — never in the background. Every dot is stored in a file on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. There is no copy of your map on any server, ours or anyone else's.

Location permission is optional and uses iOS's standard "While Using the App" access. You can revoke it at any time in Settings, and you can erase your entire map inside the app with one tap — which deletes the only copy that has ever existed.

The map view (Randhawa only)

The optional map background is provided by Apple Maps (MapKit), an iOS system service. When map tiles load, that request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy — the same as any app that shows an Apple map. Randhawa itself sends nothing. The constellation view, and the widget, work entirely offline.

Bhullar

Bhullar requests no permissions at all — no location, no contacts, no photos, nothing. It makes no network connections. Everything it shows is computed on your device from the current date and your calendar settings.

Children

Both apps are suitable for all ages and collect no data from anyone, including children.
