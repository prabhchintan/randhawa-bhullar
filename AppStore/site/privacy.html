Privacy Policy · Last updated June 2026

Randhawa does not collect any data.

What we collect

Nothing. Randhawa has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network connections.

How it works

Everything you see — the dots, the percentage, the day count — is calculated on your device from the current date and your calendar settings. None of it leaves your phone, and nothing is stored on any server.

Permissions

Randhawa requests no special permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other personal data.

Children

Randhawa is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
