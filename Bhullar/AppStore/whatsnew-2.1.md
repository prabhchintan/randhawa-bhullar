Dots you can open.

Swiping is the zoom now, which frees the tap to mean "this one". Swipe left to go finer, from months to weeks to days to hours to minutes, and right to come back out.

Then tap any dot that has already happened. Bhullar tells you what that stretch of time held: where you were during it, and what you kept from it. Two places on a Tuesday afternoon, the note you wrote that evening, the photo attached to it.

The places come from Randhawa, the sibling map app. Turn on its new trail and they fill in on their own; without it you will see the places you were when you happened to open Randhawa.

A dot still ahead of now says only "Not yet."
