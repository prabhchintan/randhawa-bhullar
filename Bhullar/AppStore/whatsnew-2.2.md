Gold in the widgets.

The days and hours that hold a memory now glow gold in the widgets too, the way they always have in the grid. Glance at the Home Screen and the year shows not only how far along it is but where you kept something.

The composer can take a photo with the camera now, next to choosing one from your library. And Bhullar and Randhawa name places through one shared lookup, so a place is the same word in both.

Randhawa, the sibling map app, draws its map by default from this release. Carry your phone and the dots you open here fill in with the places you went, on their own.
