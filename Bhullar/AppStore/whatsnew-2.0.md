# Bhullar 2.0: What's New (for App Store Connect)

Bhullar 2.0 gives the dots something to hold.

• Memories: tap the plus button and write down what right now deserves to keep, with a photo if you like. Units that hold a memory glow gold in the grid.

• On this day: when a memory's date comes around again, it resurfaces under the grid, right when it means the most.

• Together with Randhawa: if you also use our map app, memories flow both ways. Made in either, shown in both. Randhawa gives them a place; Bhullar gives them a time.

• iCloud sync, off until you turn it on: memories are kept in your private iCloud, which we cannot read, and come back when you sign in on a new phone. No account to create.

Still no tracking, no analytics, no ads.
