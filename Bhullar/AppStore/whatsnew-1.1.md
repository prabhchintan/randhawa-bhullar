# Bhullar 1.1: What's New (for App Store Connect)

New in 1.1:

• Dots at Any Scale, a new widget you can set to months, weeks, days, or hours. Edit the widget to choose. Add it twice at two scales.
• The telescope zooms deeper: a new minutes view, 1,440 dots for today. Tap the grid to reach it.
• The grid now ticks forward on its own while the app is open. Watch the day fill in.

Still no accounts, no tracking, no data collected. Just dots.
